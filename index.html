Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile applications published by Tobias Heinrich on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

1. No Data Collection by the Developer

Privacy is critically important. The developer does not collect, receive, or transmit any of your data. No data is ever sent to an external server, cloud database, or to the developer. There are no user accounts and no location tracking.

2. Local, On-Device Storage

Any information you enter into an application — which, depending on the app, may include personal data such as names, dates of birth, contact details, and emergency/guardian contacts — is stored only locally on your own device. It never leaves your device except through actions you explicitly initiate (see below). If you uninstall the app, this local data is removed.

3. User-Initiated Device-to-Device Transfer

Some applications can transfer data directly between two devices via QR codes, only when you choose to do so. This exchange happens locally between the devices and is never routed through any server or the developer.

4. Your Responsibility for Personal Data of Others

If you use an application to store personal data about other people (for example, athletes, children, or emergency contacts), you are the party responsible (controller) for that data and for having a lawful basis to process it. The application is solely a local tool that stores this data on your device.

5. Advertisements and Analytics

The applications do not include any advertisement, promotional tracking, or third-party analytical software.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the app features change in the future, this privacy policy page will be updated to clearly explain what data is handled.

7. Contact
